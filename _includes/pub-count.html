{%- comment -%} Count the entries in `items` whose facet equals `value`. `field` is either "year" or "venue"; years are read from the entry date and venues through the canonical label, the same way the entries themselves are labelled. Pass `items` as a collection. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign total = 0 -%} {%- for entry in include.items -%} {%- if include.field == "year" -%} {%- assign entry_value = entry.date | date: "%Y" -%} {%- else -%} {%- capture entry_value -%}{%- include pub-venue.html venue=entry.venue -%}{%- endcapture -%} {%- assign entry_value = entry_value | strip -%} {%- endif -%} {%- if entry_value == include.value -%} {%- assign total = total | plus: 1 -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {{- total -}}
